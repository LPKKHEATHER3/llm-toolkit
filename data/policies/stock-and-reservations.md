# Stock levels and reservations

This is a fictional policy for Pinewick Supply, a made-up store used only to test this toolkit.

## Issuing stock

Staff record every sale, transfer, or write-off as a stock movement with a reason. Stock is never edited directly. A movement that would take an item below zero is refused, and staff must count the shelf before trying again.

Common reasons are "sale", "damaged", "transfer to branch", and "staff use". A reason is required for every movement.

## Low stock

An item is low on stock when its quantity is at or below its reorder level. Low-stock items are reordered every Monday. An item with zero stock can still be shown online with the label "back soon".

## Reservations

Customers can reserve up to 5 units of an item for store pickup. A reservation is held for 48 hours. Reservations are not accepted for items that are already low on stock.

## Correcting mistakes

A wrongly recorded movement is reversed with the undo function, not by recording an opposite movement. Undo reverses only the most recent change in the system, so staff must check that the change on top of the undo list is the one they made before using it.

If the change on top of the list belongs to someone else, staff ask a supervisor instead of undoing it.

# Payments and loyalty points

This is a fictional policy for Pinewick Supply, a made-up store used only to test this toolkit.

## Accepted payments

We accept credit and debit cards, bank transfer, and QR payment. Cash is accepted only in the store. Bank transfers must be completed within 24 hours of placing the order, or the order is cancelled.

## Loyalty points

Members earn 1 point for every 25 baht spent. Points are added 14 days after delivery, once the return window for most items has passed.

Each point is worth 0.25 baht at checkout. Points can pay for up to half of an order's total. Points expire 18 months after they were earned.

Points are not earned on shipping fees, gift cards, or items bought with store credit.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in amounts of 200, 500, and 1000 baht. They do not expire and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Price matching

We match the price of an identical item sold by another store in the same city, if the item is in stock there. Price matching does not apply to clearance sale items or to online marketplaces.

# Shipping and delivery

This is a fictional policy for Pinewick Supply, a made-up store used only to test this toolkit.

## Delivery options

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 40 baht. Standard delivery is free for orders of 800 baht or more.

Express delivery arrives the next business day when the order is placed before 2 p.m. Express delivery costs 120 baht and is never free.

Store pickup is free. Pickup orders are ready within 4 hours during opening hours, and they are held for 7 days before being cancelled and refunded.

## Order processing

Orders placed on a weekend or public holiday are processed on the next business day. An order ships only when every item in it is in stock, unless the customer asks for a split shipment.

Split shipments are free when the delay is caused by our stock levels.

## Damaged or missing parcels

Report a damaged parcel within 48 hours of delivery and include a photo of the box and the item. A parcel marked as delivered but not received must be reported within 7 days.

We send a replacement or a full refund for damaged parcels, at the customer's choice.

## Addresses

The delivery address cannot be changed after an order has shipped. Before it ships, the address can be changed once from the order page.

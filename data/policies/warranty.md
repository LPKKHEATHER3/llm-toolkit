# Warranty

This is a fictional policy for Pinewick Supply, a made-up store used only to test this toolkit.

## Coverage

Electronics carry a 12-month warranty from the delivery date. Keyboards and mice carry a 24-month warranty. Stationery and office supplies are covered for manufacturing defects for 90 days.

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear of keycaps, or batteries that have lost capacity through normal use.

## Making a claim

A warranty claim needs the order number and a short description of the fault. A video of the fault speeds up the claim but is not required.

Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days. If the same item fails twice under warranty, the customer may choose a refund instead of a second replacement.

## Power banks

Power banks are covered for 12 months, but a power bank that has swollen must not be shipped. Bring a swollen power bank to the store instead; it is replaced on the spot when the claim is approved.

## Transfer

The warranty stays with the item if it is given away. The new owner needs the original order number to make a claim.

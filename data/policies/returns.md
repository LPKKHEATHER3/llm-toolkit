# Returns and refunds

This is a fictional policy for Pinewick Supply, a made-up store used only to test this toolkit.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Electronics have a shorter window: opened electronics can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Snacks and drinks cannot be returned once the seal is broken.

Items bought during the annual clearance sale are final sale and cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged.

## Condition of returned items

Unopened items are refunded in full. Opened items that work and include every accessory are refunded minus a 15% restocking fee. The restocking fee is waived when the item is faulty.

Items with missing parts, water damage, or engraving cannot be accepted.

## How to start a return

Start a return from the order page or by writing to the support desk with the order number. The support desk replies within 2 business days with a return label.

Return labels are free for faulty items. For any other return, the cost of the label is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item is inspected. Store credit is issued immediately after inspection and never expires.

Shipping fees from the original order are refunded only when the whole order is returned because of our mistake.
